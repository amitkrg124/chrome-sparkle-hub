# Privacy Policy for IRCTC Tatkal Autofill Assistant

**Last Updated:** [Insert Date]

This privacy policy applies to the IRCTC Tatkal Autofill Assistant Chrome Extension ("Extension"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your information is used.

## 1. Information We Collect
The Extension collects information that you manually enter into its settings to facilitate the autofill process. This may include:
- Passenger details (names, ages, genders, berth preferences, food preferences).
- Travel preferences (boarding stations, coach numbers).
- Payment preferences (UPI, Credit/Debit cards, IRCTC e-Wallet).
- Business booking details (GST number, company name, address).
- IRCTC login credentials (if you choose to save them).

## 2. How We Store Your Information
All information you save in the Extension is stored locally on your device using Google Chrome's local storage capabilities.
- **No External Servers:** We do not transmit, upload, or store your data on external servers, cloud databases, or third-party services.
- **Local Only:** Your data stays on the device where you installed the Extension.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
The stored information is used exclusively to automatically fill booking forms on the official IRCTC website to save you time during the booking process.

## 4. Third-Party Websites
The Extension interacts with the IRCTC website (irctc.co.in). Please refer to the official IRCTC privacy policy for information on how they handle your data once it is submitted on their platform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of IRCTC.

## 5. CAPTCHA and Booking Guarantees
The Extension does not bypass or solve CAPTCHAs. You are responsible for solving any CAPTCHAs and reviewing all filled information before submitting a booking. The Extension does not guarantee a confirmed ticket.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the Extension's listing on the Chrome Web Store or within this document.
